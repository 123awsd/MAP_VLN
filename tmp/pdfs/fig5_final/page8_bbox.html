As shown in Table II, CPTG + JTVP achieves a 73.3% SR and improves SPL from 55.1% with fixed viewpoints to 69.0%, while reducing mean trajectory length from 27.47 m to 21.02 m. Three infrastructure failures in the full configuration are counted as failures. V. CONCLUSION We presented LogicFly, a language-guided aerial navigation framework built around CPTG and JTVP. CPTG separates precedence and observation-triggered branches; JTVP selects task order and observation poses with 3D path and terminal costs within a bounded window. Verification and constrained recovery update subsequent decisions. Under a strict full-task evaluation, our method achieves strong performance on multi-target tasks with complex logic. Ablation studies further verify the role of CPTG in executing complex task logic and the contribution of JTVP to path eﬀiciency and viewpoint planning. In addition, we deploy the system on a custom quadrotor UAV and complete multi-target tasks with logical dependencies in real indoor rooms, demon- strating its practical deployability. We hope this work will encourage the development of vision-and-language navigation beyond single-goal route following toward eﬀicient closed- loop autonomous execution of tasks with complex logic.
