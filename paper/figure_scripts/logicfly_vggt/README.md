# LogicFly Fig. 1 generation

`compose_no_fpv_tilted.py` assembles the paper figure from the generated local reconstructions and RGB frames. The full-trajectory assets are produced by `run_full_trajectory.py`, `prepare_full_reference.py`, `support_full_trajectory.py`, and `render_full_scene.py`.

The final installed Fig. 1 is `../../figures/logicfly_fig1.png`. Large source videos, point clouds, model weights, and intermediate reconstructions remain outside the repository; the scripts document the reproducible assembly and fusion steps.
