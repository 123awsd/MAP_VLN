\caption{Coverage-aware semantic recovery and observation-driven map update, illustrated for one locate-target task. (1) Two local observation poses are shown in the same overhead view. After a miss, another pose reveals additional coverage (orange); previously covered samples (gray) do not contribute redundant negative evidence. Regional search belief decreases only with new negative evidence, while the surrogate armchair remains mapped. (2) Reaching the local search limit permits recovery to the mapped living-room sofa without changing the task. The focused spatial view enlarges the recovery route; the inset retains the complete apartment context. (3) Positive verification resolves the task during execution. Consistent unmatched RGB-D observations support a 3D track; after execution, the confirmed novel cushion instance is inserted into the object map while existing nodes remain. The textured mesh and RGB views are from HM3D. The route, coverage partitions, and initial memory state are illustrative, with the cushion assumed initially unmapped. The coverage panels are explanatory spatial diagrams, not calibrated camera footprints or measured confidence maps.}
